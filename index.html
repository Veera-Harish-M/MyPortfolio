End-to-end ownership — an endless loop of shipping, learning and improving.

  • 🧭 Requirements Gather & scope I translate ambiguous business problems into concrete AI/ML solution architecture — scoping GenAI & MLOps features directly with architects and stakeholders.
  • ✦ Design Architect the solution I design across every layer — frontend, backend, ML, infra & deployment — with FastAPI, MLflow, RAG, LangChain and Kubernetes, built to scale from day one.
  • ⌘ Develop Build & test Ground-up feature development with full ownership — ML inference, model-lifecycle workflows and full-stack capabilities, adopting new frameworks fast.
  • 🚀 Deploy Release to prod I own major production releases end-to-end — versioning, async processing, release coordination and post-deploy validation across cloud and edge.
  • ◉ Monitor Maintain & observe Observability with OpenTelemetry, Prometheus & Grafana — model monitoring, drift & distributed tracing. I keep systems healthy, not just shipped.
  • ∞ Fix & Iterate Next feature, repeat Root-cause analysis, bug resolution and continuous improvement — then I mentor the team and spin up the next feature. The loop never stops.
